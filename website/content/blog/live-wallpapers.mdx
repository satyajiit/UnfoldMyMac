Pulse turns system load into a moving chrome sculpture. Codex Mission Control gives coding activity a small robot. GitHub After Hours turns a public profile into a floating city. Each is a scene in UnfoldMyMac’s wallpaper library, with a visual template and optional data behind it.

Open **Dynamic Wallpapers** for twenty designs, or **Creative Scenes** for Hinge Garden and The Workshop. You can try a preview before choosing **Use wallpaper**, and you can leave the personal data connections alone until you want them. The [project overview](https://github.com/satyajiit/UnfoldMyMac#living-wallpapers) describes the collection.

## Find your design in the new library

Switch between grid and list, explore category tabs, and narrow the results with tag filters. Cards show the related brands and features a design uses. Open a card for the full description, author links, and an actual design preview. The name and usage badges sit above the preview; **Preview**, **Use wallpaper** or **Use scene**, **Customize**, and **Show Desktop** follow below. Show Desktop hides other apps and minimizes UnfoldMyMac so you can see the active desktop. Bring apps back from the Dock. The title stays in the toolbar as you scroll.

## Start with the scene

Choose a wallpaper for what you want to look at. Daydream brings in an image and floating glass. Lights Out has an open-wheel car, a flowing circuit, and a five-light countdown. Grok Event Horizon puts a black hole on your desktop.

GTA VI — Vice City Countdown puts official Rockstar artwork behind the wait. It counts calendar days in your time zone toward the console release date stored in the template. [Rockstar currently lists November 19, 2026](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI), checked September 12, 2026. The artwork is © Rockstar Games.

Aurora Observatory takes the view into orbit. Public [NOAA OVATION forecasts](https://www.swpc.noaa.gov/products/aurora-30-minute-forecast) shape the auroral curtains, while planetary Kp readings give the scene its activity level. The globe uses [NASA Earth Observatory imagery](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/). It is an artistic interpretation of space weather, with no account setup required.

The Grok and racing counters are wallpaper-session counters. They do not report Grok service usage or live race telemetry. The website’s wallpaper recordings also use sample data, so their numbers are demonstrations, not measurements of your machine.

## Creative Scenes: Hinge Garden

Hinge Garden has its own home in **Creative Scenes**. A glass snail emerges as the lid opens and retreats as it closes. Connect a charger while the scene is running and a nine-second pulse crosses the pool, follows the roots, climbs the shell, and lights the greenhouse. A softer glow remains while external power is connected, even at full battery.

Battery level, local time, and CPU activity shape the atmosphere. **Customize** has separate Motion, Sound, and One-liners controls. Pointer parallax, supported tilt and gyroscope input, and microphone reactions are optional. Audio is measured locally, never recorded or transcribed. Reduce Motion holds the garden still and pauses sound reactions.

## Creative Scenes: The Workshop

The Workshop is a warm maker’s studio inside a folding wooden case. Open your Mac and the workbenches rise, lamps unfold, and a little wind-up maker starts turning a crank. Gentle foreground movement, pointer parallax, and daylight keep the room alive. Connecting your charger sends a warm current around its brass rail.

Each ordinary open app lights a tool station, up to twelve. Finder, UnfoldMyMac, and background helpers are excluded; hidden and minimized apps still count. In **Customize**, choose **Use Desktop items…** to fill the shelves from your Desktop or another folder. Immediate files, folders, packages, and shortcuts each count once. Hidden items are excluded, and file contents are never opened. Up to twenty-four parcels are drawn, while the optional caption shows the full count.

Eight original motivating lines rotate with a soft fade every forty-five seconds. Switch the lines and counts independently, turn pointer parallax on or off, or mirror the workshop. Folder access is optional. Reduce Motion holds a complete open pose, keeps counts current, and holds the current line.

[See The Workshop’s native preview](/showcase/#the-workshop). The website recording uses sample app and parcel counts, with optional text hidden. The [scene’s source and metadata](https://github.com/satyajiit/UnfoldMyMac/tree/main/UnfoldMyMac/Sources/UnfoldMyMacKit/Resources/Wallpapers/the-workshop) describe its behavior.

## Add data when it serves the scene

Some scenes can react to Mac metrics, such as CPU load, memory estimates, and battery information. Others can use local Claude or Codex metadata or a public GitHub profile.

The visual template and data provider are separate pieces. That lets the renderer use a snapshot without needing to know how the data was collected. It also gives contributors a way to add a provider or a scene without replacing the wallpaper engine. The [extension guide](https://github.com/satyajiit/UnfoldMyMac/blob/main/UnfoldMyMac/DEVELOPING.md) explains the native architecture.

## Set playback for your Mac

Wallpapers support 30 or 60 fps playback across displays. The playback policy lowers the maximum to 30 fps under low-power or thermal limits. Sleep stops playback. Reduce Motion disables continuous animation and limits updates.

These are the app’s playback rules, not a promise that every GPU and display combination will sustain the same frame rate. The policy is visible in [WallpaperPlayback.swift](https://github.com/satyajiit/UnfoldMyMac/blob/main/UnfoldMyMac/Sources/UnfoldMyMacCore/Wallpaper/WallpaperPlayback.swift).

## Bring your own picture or snapshot

Scenes that support custom backgrounds let you import an image; GTA VI and Aurora Observatory keep their bundled artwork. You can also connect local JSON/JSONL data. Start with the built-in scenes to learn what the library already supports, then change the input that matters to you.

The app stores its own copy of imported artwork, so moving the original does not break the design. Optional connections live in **Customize** on each design page. The [data connection guide](/blog/connecting-your-data/) covers what each source reads and which ones use the network.
